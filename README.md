# Viva Homes

Viva Homes is a digital housing platform that was created to solve the problem of renting and buying homes in Ethiopia.

Our vision is an accessible housing market, where ordinary citizens can easily rent or buy properties through a digital medium.

## The Problem

The process of finding, renting, and buying homes in Ethiopia is currently challenging and flawed.

Residents have to rely on networks of dishonest and sometimes coercive middlemen who facilitate the transaction between renters/buyers and homeowners. This leads to added expenses, unregulated rates, information asymmetry, and generally inefficient processes.

In addition,

homeowners and credible real-estate agencies have the burden of shouldering a technological revolution that is supposed to ease the process of advertising, managing, and facilitating potential property transactions.

## Our Solution

Viva Homes is a digital platform that offers a centralized space where citizens can find, rent, and buy homes while limiting the intervention of unnecessary middlemen.

It will provide an avenue where users can browse available properties, find houses that meet their criteria, request more information about a property, and deal directly with the owner or agency. It also allows for the central management of all property listings and facilitates direct interaction with customers at a lower cost.

For the

real-estate agencies,

it will offer a digital space where they can advertise properties, network with potential clients, manage their listings, and manage their workloads.

It is a win-win solution where technology is used to bridge the gap of an unreliable housing system and offer more value to residents.

## Our Assumption and Vision

Viva Homes believes that real-estate agencies are critical to an efficient housing economy. It is our position that we could integrate such agencies into a reliable housing platform that would promote some level of regulation on both ends.

Viva Homes will provide the following services to the agencies integrated into the system.

• Listing property ads

• Networking capabilities

• Property management

• Credibility and verification

• Communication tools

The agencies will co-exist alongside other residents in an ecosystem where each can offer some level of value while limiting the involvement of coercive and unnecessary agents in the housing process.

## What To Expect

• Property search: Users can search and filter properties that meet their desired needs.

• Rent and purchase: The platform will support both property rental and property purchase requests.

• Property listing: Viva Homes will include an option for citizens to post property ads.

• Agency integration: The system allows for the integration of real-estate agencies into the system.

• Verification: Viva Homes will introduce a property verification system to increase the reliability of posted ads.

• Property management: Both agencies and homeowners can manage their listings easily.

• Favorites and reviews: Users can save houses and give reviews based on experience.

• Authentication: The system has a strong authentication system to protect the privacy of its users.

• Payments: The platform will include a payment option for secure transactions.

• Notifications: Users will receive notifications about activity related to their posts or bookings.

## Our Vision, Mission, And Values

Viva Homes envisions a housing market where ordinary Ethiopian residents do not have to go through a network of coercive brokers when searching for a rental or a house to buy. We believe in creating an accessible and reliable digital economy where citizens can transact with relative ease. We strive to be the bridge for every Ethiopian who wants to rent or buy a home without having to break the bank in the process.

Viva Homes – Making finding and owning homes easier in Ethiopia.
